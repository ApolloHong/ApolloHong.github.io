03 / Principles

How I see the world

01
\[p(\theta \mid \mathcal{D}) \;\propto\; p(\mathcal{D} \mid \theta)\, p(\theta)\]

Uncertainty first

“Probability theory is nothing but common sense reduced to calculation.”Pierre-Simon Laplace, 1819

Never trust a single deterministic point when the data is noisy. Keep a posterior, and update it as evidence arrives. That discipline is behind my forecasts in the QRT Data Challenge, ranked 1st on the public leaderboard.

02
\[dX_t = b(t, X_t)\,dt + \sigma(t, X_t)\,dW_t + dJ_t\]

Stochastic processes & jumps

“The market, unbeknownst to itself, obeys a law which governs it: the law of probability.”Louis Bachelier, Théorie de la spéculation, 1900

Prices are not smooth deterministic curves but stochastic paths: Brownian diffusion plus sudden jumps. With Prof. Huyên Pham I use Schrödinger bridges, an entropic stochastic-control problem, to generate SPY tail-risk scenarios under stochastic volatility and jumps.

03
\[\partial_t v(t,x) + \sup_{u}\big\{\mathcal{L}^u v(t,x) + f(t,x,u)\big\} = 0\]

Dynamic optimization

“An optimal policy has the property that whatever the initial state and initial decision are, the remaining decisions must constitute an optimal policy with regard to the state resulting from the first decision.”Richard Bellman, 1957

Executing and splitting an order is not a static fit but a Bellman problem: trading off transient market impact against inventory risk, step by step, over a multi-dimensional state.

04
\[I_t = Y \sigma \sqrt{\frac{Q}{V}}\]

Market friction & net alpha

“Noise trading provides camouflage which conceals his trading from market makers.”Albert S. Kyle, Continuous Auctions and Insider Trading, 1985

Every trade leaves a footprint. Impact grows like the square root of size (the square-root law, Tóth, Bouchaud et al.), so alpha measured before friction is an illusion; net-of-costs performance is the only criterion that survives.
