Among all diffusions that turn noise into data, the bridge picks the one closest to Brownian motion. The static problem is solved with log-domain Sinkhorn, the same IPF algorithm I implemented at Polytechnique. Pairs are then drawn from the coupling and joined with Brownian bridges. With small ε the paths approach straight optimal-transport lines. With large ε they become diffuse and the coupling loses its structure.

De Bortoli et al., Diffusion Schrödinger Bridge, NeurIPS 2021 · Cuturi, Sinkhorn distances, 2013.

This is a game between an insider who knows the value v, a crowd of noise traders, and a competitive market maker who sets the price P = E[v | order flow]. In equilibrium the insider trades gradually and hides inside the noise. The price becomes a Brownian bridge to v. More noise trading gives a deeper market (smaller λ) and a larger insider profit, yet information still arrives at exactly the same linear rate.

Kyle, Continuous auctions and insider trading, Econometrica 1985 · Back, Insider trading in continuous time, RFS 1992.
